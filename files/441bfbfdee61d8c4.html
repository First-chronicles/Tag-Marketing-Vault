3What's working

  • Business Profile: the #1 lead source (24 leads, 5 booked, $27,590 in 30 days), with 123 reviews at 4.99★.
  • Brand searches: clicks up 50% (21 vs 14).
  • Last 7 days: Google clicks jumped to 19 from 6.
  • City pages moving up: Mesa (position 48 to 39), Tempe (31 to 25), Sun City (34 to 24) and San Tan Valley (33 to 25), likely from the late-August city page updates.

4What's slipping

  • Page 1 keywords: down from 59 to 33, which is why non-brand clicks fell.
  • Buckeye: impressions halved (733 to 360) after the Sep 1 rewrite dropped the "pipe break" wording. That section was added back Sep 15.
  • Florence: "florence pipe break repair" slipped from #4 to #15. The page mentions "pipe break" only once.
  • Epoxy pipe lining page: impressions fell from 125 to 14.
  • 7 blog pages lost most of their impressions. Checked Sep 17: 3 are no longer in Google (cost guide, what is trenchless pipe repair, tree roots) and 4 are indexed but near zero. They need improving or merging before asking Google to re-index.
  • Business Profile directions: down 40%, and no new reviews since Jul 5.
