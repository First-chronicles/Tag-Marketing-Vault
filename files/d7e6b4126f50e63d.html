Blog keyword positions

Which Pipeliners blog posts show up on Google page 1, 2 and 3, and for which searches. Click a tile to switch the list.

How to read it: Page 1 = position 1 to 10, page 2 = 11 to 20, page 3 = 21 to 30. Position is Google's average for that search over the window, so a keyword seen only once or twice can jump around.
Seen once: the post showed up for that search a single time in 28 days. Treat those positions as luck, not a ranking. The keywords seen 2+ times are the ones to act on.
Other companies' names: searches for another company's name are hidden by default and left out of the counts. They are not traffic Pipeliners can win.
Not shown: searches Google hides for privacy, keywords past page 3 (they count in the "By blog" view), and blogs with no impressions in the window.
