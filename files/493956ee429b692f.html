Section 1

Executive summary

Summit Plumbing Pros runs a 200-page Arizona site covering ten East Valley cities. When I came onto it in June 2026 it had no blog, two competing designs, 780 broken icons, wrong city names on live pages and service claims the business could not honour. #07#09#12

This phase was remediation, not growth. The work was to make the site consistent, accurate and indexable so that ranking work has something solid to stand on. The figures below are what that produced by September 23. One result is worth reading first: in the local map pack Summit already places 3rd in its own area for emergency plumbing, holding 13 of 39 top-3 pins, and that channel is producing the bookings the website is not.#16

On search performance, be precise

Comparing two matching 30-day windows, June 1 to 30 against August 24 to September 22: clicks from Google rose from 28 to 38, click-through rate from 0.2 to 0.3 percent, and average position improved slightly from 28.0 to 26.9. Impressions fell from 17,800 to 11,400.#05

That is a genuine improvement in a 30-day window, and it is not the same thing as proof that the work caused it. A new website launched on September 3, inside that window. Section 5 sets out exactly what this report can and cannot claim.

Section 2

What we found

Every item below was found on the live site and recorded in a dated work report.

Critical

780 icons were broken across 116 pages. The image files were still on the server, but their Media Library records had been deleted, so the site drew empty boxes. This was also what made page spacing look uneven.#09

Critical

The site was running two different designs. Drain Cleaning used the Summit brand colours and fonts. Almost every other page was still on Elementor's out-of-the-box defaults: a different blue, black text, and Roboto instead of Poppins and Montserrat.#09

Critical

Pages promised service the business does not offer. Ten emergency city pages advertised after-hours, late-night, holiday and "2 AM" service plus flat-rate pricing, while the same pages showed Monday to Saturday, 8 to 5. One promised arrival "within the hour".#13

Critical

Every page told Google the wrong opening hours. The page data said open seven days, 9 to 5, and the About page showed a third different set. None matched the Google Business Profile.#12

Critical

Seven pages named the wrong city. Six pipe lining city pages carried the wrong location, for example the Mesa page read "Pipe lining in Chandler". The Fountain Hills emergency page said "Apache Junction".#12#13

Critical

A pricing page was live carrying template placeholder text and software subscription plans that had nothing to do with plumbing.#12

Warning

Only 4 of 176 indexed pages were properly configured in Rank Math. Focus keywords were missing from SEO titles across the site.#10

Warning

Thirteen pages carried two H1 headings, the theme's generic title bar plus the real page heading.#08

Warning

The About page carried hidden Utah business information, an empty "(Lic. #)" with no number, and three pages used "guaranteed" wording that was not approved.#11

Warning

The site had no blog at all, and the theme had no single-post layout, so featured images would not have displayed even if posts existed.#07

Warning

Some city pages were near copies of each other, two measured at 70 percent identical, and authority outside the site scored 9 out of 100.#11

Section 3

The work

Grouped by workstream. Every item comes from a dated daily work report.

Accuracy and compliance

  • Rewrote the after-hours, late-night, holiday and "2 AM" claims out of 10 emergency city pages, and removed flat-rate pricing and the "within the hour" promise.#13
  • Corrected the opening hours in the page data sitewide to Monday to Saturday 8 to 5, matching the Google Business Profile.#12
  • Fixed the wrong city name on 7 pages.#12#13
  • Took the placeholder pricing page private. Nothing linked to it.#12
  • Replaced pricing answers in FAQs with a free-estimate call to action.#08#13
  • Removed 3 competitor mentions and one low-authority link from draft content before it went live.#07

Design consistency

  • Moved 142 pages onto the Drain Cleaning design: Summit colours, Poppins and Montserrat, consistent spacing. Every page kept its own wording, links, permalink and SEO settings.#09
  • Restored 64 media records, which brought back 780 icons across 116 pages without editing a single page.#09
  • Rebuilt 7 outdated hero sections to the standard layout with breadcrumbs, intro copy and buttons.#08#09

On-page SEO

  • Built a Rank Math tracker covering all 200 pages and posts against Google's indexed list.#10
  • Wrote new titles, focus keywords and meta descriptions for 29 indexed pages where the main keyword was missing from the title, checked against the approved service and city list before applying.#10
  • Removed the duplicate H1 from 13 pages.#08
  • Added a 5-question FAQ with FAQ schema to the sewer line repair page.#08
  • Added 5 internal links into pages already sitting close to page one.#13

Content and indexing

  • Built the blog from zero: 5 posts with focus keyphrases, metadata, internal links, quick-answer openings, FAQ sections and branded banners.#07
  • Built an Elementor single-post template, which was why featured images had never displayed.#07
  • Audited indexing twice. By September 22, of 53 URLs Search Console listed as not indexed, only 10 were real pages. The rest were feeds, plugin files, site-search URLs, redirects and deliberate noindex.#14
  • Cut the sewer line repair page from 230KB to 92KB of page data and requested re-indexing.#08
In detail

780 broken icons that looked like a design problem

Symptom
Across the site, icons were missing and spacing looked uneven and amateurish. The obvious reading was that the pages had been built badly.#09
Diagnosis
It was not a design fault. The image files were still on the server, but their records in the WordPress Media Library had been deleted. Every widget pointing at a deleted record drew an empty box, and the empty boxes were what distorted the spacing.#09
Why it mattered
Editing 116 pages by hand would have taken days and risked breaking layouts. The fault was not in the pages at all.
What followed
Restoring 64 media records brought back all 780 icons at once, with no page edited and no layout touched.#09

Summit places third in its own area for emergency plumbing

Across the whole 1-mile grid Summit ranks 3rd, behind only Mr. Rooter Phoenix (average 1.6) and Iconic Plumbing (2.3). It holds 13 of 39 pins in the top 3 and has been stable at 4.6 across both September scans.#16

It also carries 520 reviews at 4.8, more than any competitor in that grid. This lines up with the CRM: the Business Profile produced 13 leads and 7 of the 12 booked jobs in the September snapshot. The map pack, not organic search, is where Summit currently earns.#12

The Mesa grid is a different story

Its average improved from 20.1 in July to 12.7 now, but that is movement inside the zone nobody clicks. Zero of 39 pins reach the top 3, 38 of 39 sit between positions 8 and 20, and Summit does not rank at all on the centre pin.#16

The grid is owned by Mesa Plumbing Co (average 1.0, top 3 on all 39 pins) and Hansen Family (2.1, all 39). Two of the top five are name matches: "Mesa Plumbing Co" and "Mesa Plumbing Pros". An improving average here should not be reported as a win.

Section 5

Candor: what the numbers do not say yet

Four limits, stated plainly, so nothing in this report can be read as more than it is.

A longer window reads differently

The 30-day comparison above shows clicks up 36 percent. Measured across the full 90 days since the work began, against the 90 days before it, clicks are down, 145 to 131, and page-one rankings fell from 83 to 61.#15

Both readings are real. June was a weak month, which flatters the 30-day comparison. Anyone reviewing this should know the longer view exists before they see it elsewhere.

A week of September traffic is missing

The relaunch was restored from a copy of Summit's Utah site, so the Analytics connection came with it. From the afternoon of September 3 to September 10, Arizona traffic was recorded in the Utah property. Reconnected September 12.#06

The traffic is not lost, it is filed in the wrong place. Every September visitor figure here is about a week short.

The relaunch sits inside the results window

A new website went live on September 3 and 86 new pages went live the day before. That is far too much change at once to attribute the search movement to any single cause, including this work.

September is a transition month. The first clean comparison is October.

The speed work cannot be proved

Images and scripts were optimised on September 16. No speed measurement was taken beforehand, and Google publishes no real-visitor speed data for this site because it does not yet get enough traffic.#02#03

The work may well have helped. It cannot be shown. From the next change onward, a before reading is taken every time.

Section 6

Next phase

These are plans, not results, so they carry no evidence tags.

First

  1. Verify lead tracking against the rotating phone pool and the CRM, so the next report can connect search to booked jobs.
  2. Push the 218 terms sitting between positions 4 and 20 onto page one. Two pages already rank between 9 and 14 and take no clicks at all, which points to something else taking the click rather than a ranking problem.
  3. Give "emergency plumber tempe" its own page. It is the largest non-brand search the site appears for, 2,495 impressions and zero clicks, currently answered by a general services page.
  4. Back the emergency grid, not the Mesa grid. Summit already holds 13 of 39 top-3 pins on emergency plumbing and none on "plumber mesa az". Effort spent on the grid that is already converting will return faster than effort spent fighting two name-matched competitors for a grid where no pin reaches the top 3.

Then

  1. Diagnose the home page position drop and report to Scott. It is off limits for edits and it carries roughly two thirds of all clicks.
  2. Recover the missing week of September analytics from the Utah property.
  3. Work down the 10 pages still not indexed.
  4. Build presence off the site. Authority scores 9 out of 100 and Summit appears in none of the 10 tracked AI-assistant answers unless the question names it.
