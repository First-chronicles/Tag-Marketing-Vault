Pipeliners SEO Results Scorecard

Did our SEO changes bring more Google traffic? For every page we changed since Sep 1, this compares its Google numbers before and after the change. Updated every Monday.

Waiting for data

Search Console runs 3 to 5 days behind, and a change needs at least 14 days of data before we can call it. Until then each table shows the locked "before" numbers (the 28 days before go-live), so the comparison is fixed in advance.

How to read this

  • Per day: the before and after windows are different lengths, so we compare impressions per day.
  • Against the whole site: Up or Down means the page moved at least 15 points more than the whole site did in the same windows. Anything less is Flat.
  • Under 60 impressions in the before window (about 2 a day): too small to call either way.
  • Clicks are what count. A better position isn't a win unless clicks follow.
  • Scope: website pages only. Google Business Profile clicks land on the homepage and aren't counted here; they're part of the Oct 9 baseline review.
