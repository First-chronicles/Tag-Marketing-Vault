No-Dig Pipe Restoration

How Trenchless Pipe Lining Works

Instead of digging up and hauling out your old sewer pipe, we rebuild it from the inside. The result is a brand-new, seamless pipe formed within your existing line, with no trench across the yard.

No Digging, No Demolition

We restore the line through existing access points. No trench across the lawn, no jackhammered basement slab, no torn-up driveway.

Often 20 to 30% Less

With little to no excavation, trenchless typically costs far less than dig-and-replace once you add up the landscaping, concrete, and restoration you'd otherwise redo.

Protects Your Property

Mature trees, patios, flower beds, and hardscape stay put. Matching old tile or finishes after a dig is often impossible, so trenchless avoids the problem entirely.

Done in Days, Not Weeks

Most residential lining jobs wrap in a day or two, versus the week-plus a full excavation and yard restoration can drag on.

Better Flow, Stronger Pipe

The new liner is seamless and smooth-walled, so it flows better than aged pipe and shrugs off the root intrusion that cracks old clay and cast iron.

Built to Last 50+ Years

Cured-in-place pipe is engineered for a 50-year-plus service life, and we back our lining work with a 20-year warranty. A permanent fix, not a patch.

When to Get Your Line Scoped

Sewer problems rarely fix themselves, they escalate. If you are seeing any of these signs, it is time to get a camera in the line before it turns into a dig-up emergency:

Sewage backing up into drains or tubs
More than one slow drain at once
Gurgling toilets or drains
Sewage smell inside or in the yard
Backups that keep coming back after snaking
Old clay or cast iron sewer pipe
Soggy spots or sinkholes in the yard

What is trenchless (CIPP) pipe lining? +
CIPP stands for cured-in-place pipe. We insert a resin-saturated liner into your existing sewer pipe and cure it in place, creating a seamless new pipe inside the old one. It rebuilds the line without digging a trench across your yard. We use the HammerHead Bluelight LED curing system for a fast, strong cure.
Do you have to dig up my yard? +
In most cases, no. That is the whole point of trenchless. With CIPP lining and pipe bursting we repair or replace the line through existing access points, so your lawn, driveway, and landscaping stay intact. After the camera inspection we’ll tell you exactly which method fits your line.

A CIPP-lined pipe is designed to last 50+ years, and we back our lining work with a 20-year warranty. It is a permanent fix, not a patch. You see the condition of your line on camera first, so you know exactly what you are paying to solve.
