How each term was judged

A term earns Keep only if it survives all five tests: it matches the page topic, names a service Pipeliners actually performs, fits the target keyword's search intent, appears naturally in the surrounding copy, and makes sense for an Arizona service market.

Review means defensible but conditional — usually a term that belongs on some pages but not this one, or one that depends on a service decision only you can make.

Remove means it fails at least one test outright.

Done marks terms already unapproved in OTTO. They're listed so the record is complete and so over-removals stay visible.

1 · Major problems found

Brand-name entity collision is the root cause of the worst terms. "Pipeliners" collides with Pipeliners Cloud (welding apparel) and with the oil & gas pipeline trade. That single ambiguity produced nineteen homepage terms — welding, american welding society, revco, pumco pipeline, pipefitter — none of which have anything to do with the business. It will recur unless the entity signal is strengthened.

City names resolve to the wrong place. "Buckeye" returned the Ohio State mascot and spring semester. This is the same failure mode as the brand collision, applied to geography.

Page furniture is being scraped as vocabulary. skip to content, required, email address, request service, free quote, schedule service. These come from navigation, buttons, and form validation — not content.

Out-of-service terms cluster around adjacent trades. Water heaters appeared on six pages, gas lines on two, faucets on one. OTTO infers "plumbing company" from context and fills in the standard plumbing service menu, most of which Pipeliners doesn't sell.

City pages cannibalize each other. Phoenix carried tempe, paradise valley, and peoria; San Tan Valley carries queen creek; Paradise Valley carries scottsdale. Each of those cities has its own page.

Bare verbs and fragments inflate the count without adding meaning. repair, repaired, replaced, installation, serve, cured, valley, professional. Roughly forty terms across the site are this class.

And the cleanup itself over-reached twice — nineteen unapproved homepage terms beyond the approved list, and six good inspection terms removed from the Mesa guide. Worth knowing that the correction needs correcting.
