Blog focus keywords: aligned and live

Every live blog post now targets one focus keyword, and that keyword appears in its page title, search description, main heading, first 100 words and at least one subheading. Keywords were chosen from each post's own Google searches (last 90 days) plus search volume and difficulty. Slugs were not changed. Checked live on desktop and Googlebot mobile on Sep 24, 2026.

How it was done

  • Picked one keyword per post from its real Google searches, and avoided the head terms our service pages already own (hydro jetting, leak detection, trenchless sewer repair).
  • Where the keyword was missing up top, added a short "Quick answer" that answers the search in 2 or 3 sentences, using only facts already in the post.
  • Added or renamed one subheading so it uses the keyword. New headings are the blue #1a446f.
  • Updated the title, description and Facebook/X share text in OTTO, then refreshed each page one at a time.
  • No em dashes, no new prices, no competitor names, phone numbers untouched.
  • Every post has a saved copy of its old title and content, so any one of them can be put back.
