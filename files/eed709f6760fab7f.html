The short answer

Two different things are going on. The big drop in Google "impressions" since Sep 17 is mostly not real customers: it is page-5-and-deeper listings that almost nobody sees, and the same thing happened in September last year. The drop that does matter is clicks, and most of those were lost from the Google Business Profile listing, not the website's rankings.

Watch, not an emergency

Impressions down 46%

Almost all of the loss is at ranking position 50+. Page-1 impressions held steady and rankings did not move. The site was not penalized.

On hold

Business Profile clicks down 58%

The listing was shown just as often (1,198 vs 1,201 views), but website clicks fell from 38 to 16 and calls from 22 to 16. Skipped for now; kept here so it stays tracked.

Needs action

About 3.3 clicks a day in May, about 2 a day in summer, 0.9 a day since Sep 17. Few pages rank on page 1, and every mobile page loads slowly.

What this tells us

The Business Profile link was shown more after Sep 17, not less, yet its clicks fell by three quarters. The branded search "pipeliners usa" still ranks #1 but went from 11 clicks to 0. People are still finding the business; fewer of them are tapping through. City and blog pages lost impressions, but they were getting almost no clicks before either, so that part of the drop costs very little.
