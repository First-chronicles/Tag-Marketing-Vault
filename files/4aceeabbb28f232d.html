Overall condition

The site is technically healthy but commercially invisible. Canonicals, robots directives, HTTPS handling, image alt text, and internal link depth are all in good shape, and there are no orphaned content pages. The problem is not crawling or indexing. It is that the site earns almost no clicks from the traffic it already qualifies for.

Over the 90 days to July 23, 2026, Google Search Console recorded 57,630 impressions and 95 clicks across the property, a site-wide click-through rate of roughly 0.16 percent at an average position of 35.9. At page level the concentration is stark: the homepage accounts for 27 of those clicks and /contact/ for one, while the largest page on the site, /glendale/, drew 3,449 impressions and zero clicks. /phoenix/ drew 2,360 impressions and zero clicks. Of 2,760 ranking queries, the only one producing meaningful clicks is the brand name “pipeliners.”

The most important issue found

The Rank Math SEO plugin is deactivated. Every SEO title and meta description stored in WordPress is inert. The live tags are served entirely by the Search Atlas OTTO pixel, and on the 43 URLs OTTO had not yet written a title for, the page fell back to the raw WordPress title plus a site-name suffix. That is why core commercial pages such as /trenchless-sewer-repair/ and /whole-house-repipe/ were serving 74 to 97 character titles that truncate in search results.

This was verified directly rather than assumed: /whole-house-repipe/ stores a Rank Math description reading “Fix gurgling drains with expert tips & advice” (wrong content, copied from another post) while serving an entirely different OTTO description on the live page. A LiteSpeed cache purge confirmed this is not a caching artifact.

Work completed

Twenty priority pages, about 19 percent of the 104 real content URLs, were selected on business value combined with Search Console evidence. For each, the SEO title and meta description were rewritten and deployed live through OTTO, which is the layer actually serving the tags. All 40 changes were verified on the live site after a cache purge, and all 20 pages passed a regression check covering canonicals, robots directives, heading counts, word counts, forms, and tracking.

Expected benefit

The work targets click-through rate and relevance, which is the specific failure mode the data shows. Twelve of the twenty pages previously served titles that truncate in results; those now display in full. Two pages had descriptions that described the wrong service entirely. Pages such as /goodyear/ at position 24 and /buckeye/ at position 30 are close enough to page one that title relevance can plausibly move them.

No ranking or traffic improvement is claimed. Title and description changes affect how a result is presented, not its position directly. Re-measure in Search Console on August 21, 2026 (4 weeks) and September 18, 2026 (8 weeks) against the baseline in this report. Impressions and average position for the 20 changed pages should be compared against the unchanged city pages, which act as a natural control group.

Access and data limitations

  • Titles and descriptions could not be set in WordPress. With Rank Math inactive, editing rank_math_title would have had no effect. All changes were made through OTTO instead, which is why the deployment channel differs from a standard WordPress engagement.
  • Search Console suppresses low-volume rows. Six of the twenty priority pages report no impression data, which normally means they fall below the reporting threshold rather than that they receive no impressions.
  • Four July 2026 monsoon posts are not yet in OTTO’s index, so they could not be given descriptions in this pass. They currently serve none at all.
  • Position data is a 90-day average and will understate pages that moved sharply within the window.
  • Content edits, heading fixes, internal link repairs, and redirect changes were deliberately left as recommendations. They carry structural risk and are listed in Section 8.

How priority pages were selected

The sitemap holds 121 URLs. Removing 15 archive URLs and 2 theme template records leaves 104 real content pages. The brief called for the top 10 to 20 percent, so 20 pages were selected, which is 19 percent. Selection weighted five factors:

  1. Revenue proximity. Core service pages and the free inspection conversion page were included regardless of current volume, because they are where leads are actually captured.
  2. Impressions already earned. Pages Google already shows are the cheapest to improve. /glendale/, /phoenix/, /tempe/ and /buckeye/ qualified on this alone.
  3. Striking distance. Pages ranking in positions 20 to 40 were preferred over deeper ones. /goodyear/ at position 24 is the best-positioned location page on the site.
  4. Severity of the on-page defect. Pages with no managed title, a truncating title, an intent-mismatched description, or multiple H1s were prioritized.
  5. Deliberate exclusions. The 31 lower-volume city pages were excluded because their content was already de-duplicated in the July 2026 pass and they are better handled as a batch. Archives, legal pages, and template records were excluded as low value.

A note on scope discipline. The site has 48 blog posts. Only three were selected, because blog content is not where this site loses money. The click gap sits on commercial service and location pages, so that is where the work was concentrated.
