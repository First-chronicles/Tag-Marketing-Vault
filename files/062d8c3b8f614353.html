Restore Commercial Lines Without Tearing Up the Property

CIPP Lining and Pipe Bursting for Commercial Systems

Trenchless pipelining restores a commercial sewer or drain line without excavating floors, parking lots, or landscaping. The two methods we use are cured-in-place pipe (CIPP) lining and pipe bursting.

CIPP lining installs a resin liner inside the existing pipe and cures it into a seamless new pipe wall, sealing cracks, corroded joints, and root intrusion, and it works on the larger-diameter lines common in commercial buildings. Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE pipe through a failed line when the old pipe is too far gone to line. Both avoid the excavation that shuts a business down, and a camera inspection determines which the line needs. Trenchless is not right for every line, so eligibility is confirmed before any work begins.

Signs a Colorado Springs Commercial Sewer Line Needs Attention

Backups That Affect Tenants and Operations

Recurring Backups Across Units or Restrooms

When a backup affects more than one unit or restroom, the problem is in the shared main, not a single fixture, and it will keep returning until the line is addressed.

Grease-Line Trouble in a Kitchen

Restaurant and commercial-kitchen lines coat with grease and scale, causing slow drains and backups that jetting and lining resolve.

Slow Drains Building-Wide

Multiple slow fixtures across a property point to a restriction in the main lateral serving the building.

A Line Flagged During Inspection or Sale

A camera inspection during due diligence or a facility audit often reveals cracks, offsets, or root intrusion that need rehabilitation.

Repeated Emergency Calls

If you are paying for the same line to be cleared again and again, the pipe likely has a structural problem that cleaning alone will not fix.

Commercial Sewer Challenges in Colorado Springs

A few local factors shape commercial sewer work in Colorado Springs.

Permitting and downtime. Commercial building work in the city is permitted through the Pikes Peak Regional Building Department, and commercial plan review commonly runs 20 to 30 business days, so an unplanned excavation that closes a business carries real schedule and revenue cost.

Older commercial cores. Downtown and the older commercial corridors have buildings whose sewer laterals have been in the ground for decades, where clay and cast iron scale, corrode, and take on roots.

Trenchless keeps the property open. Lining or bursting the line from existing access avoids tearing up a parking lot, slab, or storefront. For commercial permitting, see the Pikes Peak Regional Building Department.

Trenchless Pipelining Versus Excavation

Consideration
Minimal Disruption Trenchless Sewer Repair
Conventional Method Traditional Sewer Repair
Excavation ✓ Uses existing or limited access points when possible • May require a trench along the pipe route
Property Impact ✓ Helps limit damage to landscaping and concrete • May require major surface restoration
Diagnosis ✓ Begins with a sewer camera inspection • Excavation may be needed to expose the line

For a commercial property, the real cost of open-trench replacement is not only the dig. It is the closed business, the torn-up parking lot, and the restoration afterward. Excavation still has its place for a fully collapsed line, but the disruption is significant. Trenchless pipelining aims for the same structural result while keeping the property open, when the line qualifies. The camera inspection is what decides which approach the line actually needs.

Commercial Trenchless Pipelining Colorado Springs, CO: Frequently Asked Questions

Can you reline a commercial line without closing the business?
In most cases, yes. Because trenchless rehabilitation works from access points instead of a trench, we plan the work to keep restrooms, kitchens, and parking areas usable. We confirm the plan after we scope the line.
Does CIPP lining work on larger commercial pipe?
Yes. Cured-in-place lining is well suited to the larger-diameter and longer runs common in commercial and multi-tenant buildings, sealing the entire run rather than patching one section.
Our restaurant line keeps backing up with grease. Can you help?
Yes. We hydro jet the line to clear grease and scale, and if the pipe is cracked or root-invaded, CIPP lining seals it. A camera inspection shows which the line needs.
What if the line is too damaged to line?
If a line has fully collapsed or badly offset, pipe bursting can pull a new pipe through the old path, or a targeted excavation may be needed. The inspection tells us which.
Can you inspect a line for a property sale or facility audit?
Yes. A camera inspection during due diligence documents the condition of the sewer line so there are no surprises after closing.
Do you serve property managers throughout Colorado Springs?
Yes, across Colorado Springs and the surrounding El Paso County area, working with property managers, HOAs, and facility managers.
