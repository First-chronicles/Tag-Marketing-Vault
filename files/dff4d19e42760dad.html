The strategy in three plays

In order of impact on calls
PLAY A · MAP PACK

Win the map pack around Mesa

8 of the top 10 map ranking factors are about the Google Business Profile: the primary category, being open when people search, the star rating, and the number of reviews with text. Recent reviews count more than the total.

  • Steady weekly review requests, with no filtering and no rewards
  • A personal reply to every review within 2 days
  • Accurate services, categories and hours
PLAY B · MONEY PAGES

Make fewer pages worth ranking

"A dedicated page for each service" is the #1 organic factor for local businesses. The general plumbing pages for Mesa, Gilbert, Chandler and Tempe, plus the 15 service pages, are where the search volume is. The ~120 city pages get sorted in October using real data.

  • Build the Mesa page into the page that deserves "plumber mesa az" (the homepage stays off limits)
  • Connect service pages and city pages with links
  • Keep, merge or noindex: decided one page at a time, with approval
PLAY C · TRUST & AI ANSWERS

Be the source AI answers quote

Google shows an AI Overview on 92 to 97% of cost and how-to questions, but on only 15% of "plumber near me" style searches. AI tools favor businesses with proof of real work and mentions on other trusted sites.

  • How-to and "what affects the cost" guides based on real Summit jobs (no prices)
  • ROC license, real photos and real people shown on the page
  • Matching listings on Bing, Apple, Yelp and Facebook

Phase 1 · Profile and reviews

Low risk and quick to show results. None of this touches the website.

  1. 1. Reply to the 24 unreplied reviews

    A short personal reply to each, naming the job and the tech where the review does. From now on, reply within 2 days.

    Why: 81% of consumers expect a reply within a week, and 50% dislike templated replies (BrightLocal, Feb 2026). Google's rater guidelines treat reputation as a trust signal.

    No site changeApprovalKim approves each reply (Search Atlas can draft them)MeasureUnreplied reviews: 24 to 0

  2. 2. Restart the review requests

    After every finished job, the customer gets a review request text from GHL. Turn off any star filter that only sends happy customers to Google. No discounts in exchange for reviews.

    Why: Rankings can slip after about 3 weeks without new reviews, and reviews with text beat star-only ratings (Sterling Sky). Filtering or rewarding reviews breaks Google policy and the FTC review rule.

What we won't do

Popular tactics that are risky or already outdated

Add more city pagesGoogle names city pages that only funnel visitors as doorway abuse, and new pages with swapped city names are what spam updates target.

Add "Mesa" or other keywords to the profile nameIt ranks well for the #1 competitor, but it breaks Google's profile rules and can get the listing suspended.

Reward reviews or filter who gets askedGoogle can block new reviews or put a warning on the profile, and the FTC rule bans buying reviews.

Chase FAQ rich resultsGoogle stopped showing them on May 7, 2026. FAQs stay on the pages for readers and AI answers, but adding more FAQ schema won't pay off.

Bulk-deploy OTTO or turn the pixel on1,993 queued fixes include 24/7 claims and fake offers. Rank Math stays the only system that changes SEO settings.

Buy linksIn a documented case, the Aug 2025 spam update wiped out a local business's organic rankings because of paid backlinks.

Change the homepageIt's off limits by Scott's rule. It is also the page the map listing sends people to and the page Google shows for "plumber mesa az". Any homepage idea goes to Scott as a report only.

Make big content changes before Oct 2Otherwise nobody can tell whether the redesign or our edits caused a change.
