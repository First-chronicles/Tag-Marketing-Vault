Warning Signs Your Sewer Line May Be Failing

Know What to Watch for at Your Boone Home

Sewer issues often start subtly before becoming urgent. If you notice these problems around your Boone property, it may be time to schedule an inspection and find out whether underground pipe repair, residential sewer repair, or a trenchless solution is the right next step.

Multiple Drains Running Slow

If sinks, tubs, and toilets are draining slowly at the same time, the issue may be deeper in the main sewer line rather than at a single fixture.

Recurring Backups or Sewer Odors

Frequent backups or foul smells inside or outside the home can point to a damaged line that needs attention before the problem worsens.

Wet Spots or Soggy Areas Outside

Unexplained soft ground, greener patches of grass, or standing water in the yard may be signs of a leaking sewer line below the surface.

If you are noticing one or more of these signs, a sewer camera inspection is usually the best way to confirm what is happening inside the pipe

How Our Trenchless Process Works

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers For Homeowners

How much does trenchless sewer repair in Boone cost?

Trenchless sewer repair cost depends on the pipe length, depth, diameter, condition, access points, and severity of the damage. A sewer camera inspection is the best way to confirm the issue and provide an accurate written estimate.

Is no-dig sewer repair available in Boone?

Yes. Alphalete Trenchless Services provides no-dig and minimal-dig sewer repair options in Boone. A camera inspection confirms whether your existing pipe is a good candidate for CIPP lining.

Will trenchless sewer repair require digging?

Some projects may require a small access point, but trenchless sewer repair is designed to avoid full-length excavation whenever possible.

Can trenchless lining fix root intrusion?

Yes, in many cases. Roots are removed first, then the liner seals cracks and joints where roots commonly enter the pipe. The inspection determines whether lining is the right repair.

How long does trenchless sewer lining take?

Many residential trenchless sewer lining projects can be completed in one to two days, depending on pipe length, access, cleaning needs, and project complexity.

How long does a CIPP sewer liner last?

A properly installed CIPP liner is commonly rated for 50+ years and Alphalete backs CIPP pipe lining with a 20-year warranty.

What is the first step if I have a sewer problem?

The first step is to schedule a sewer camera inspection. This lets Alphalete identify the cause of the problem and recommend the best repair option before work begins.
