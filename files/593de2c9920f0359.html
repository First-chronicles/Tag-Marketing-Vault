Final decision

What is helping Summit appear organically?
The Mesa Business Profile (517 reviews, 4.8 rating, 13 CRM leads and 7 bookings in 28 days), brand searches, and pages such as Fountain Hills drain (position 8), /slab-leaks/ and Queen Creek tankless.
What is preventing further growth?
City pages that lost broad queries after the Sep 3 relaunch, near-identical Chandler and Mesa copy, parent pages outranking city pages, 28 pages not indexed, and organic leads that are few and not booking.
Fastest opportunity pages and cities
Queen Creek water heater queries (position 12 to 18), Tempe emergency routing to /emergency-plumber-in-tempe/, Apache Junction leak pages (8 to 10), /sewer-line-repair-mesa/ (18).
What should the SEO team work on first?
Business Profile cleanup and schema hours, then the 3 organic CRM leads and the not-indexed list.
Monitor in the next 7 days
GA4 sessions daily, clicks on pages changed Sep 15, CRM organic and GBP leads, GBP calls vs views.
