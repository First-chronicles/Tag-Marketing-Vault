The short answer

Two different things are going on. The big drop in Google "impressions" since Sep 17 is mostly not real customers: it is page-5-and-deeper listings that almost nobody sees, and the same thing happened in September last year. The drop that does matter is clicks, and most of those were lost from the Google Business Profile listing, not the website's rankings.

Watch, not an emergency

Impressions down 46%

Almost all of the loss is at ranking position 50+. Page-1 impressions held steady and rankings did not move. The site was not penalized.

Needs action

Business Profile clicks down 58%

The listing was shown just as often (1,198 vs 1,201 views), but website clicks fell from 38 to 16 and calls from 22 to 16.

Needs action

Clicks have been sliding since May

About 3.3 clicks a day in May, about 2 a day in summer, 0.9 a day since Sep 17. Few pages rank on page 1, and every mobile page loads slowly.

Healthy

The site itself is sound

All 116 pages load, Google reports no crawl problems, every page has one H1, a canonical and schema. Nothing technical explains the drop.

What this tells us

The Business Profile link was shown more after Sep 17, not less, yet its clicks fell by three quarters. The branded search "pipeliners usa" still ranks #1 but went from 11 clicks to 0. People are still finding the business; fewer of them are tapping through. City and blog pages lost impressions, but they were getting almost no clicks before either, so that part of the drop costs very little.

Technical audit

Crawled all 116 sitemap pages as Googlebot on a phone, checked 367 link targets, and read Google's own crawl, indexing and speed reports.

GoodCrawlingGoogle reports "No problems" on every host. 88% of Google's requests return OK, and all 116 pages return 200.
GoodTitles, H1s and canonicalsEvery page has exactly one H1 and a self-referencing canonical. Only 1 title is over 60 characters and 1 is duplicated.
GoodStructured data and sharing tagsAll 116 pages carry schema, og:image and a mobile viewport. No mixed http content.
GoodImage alt text20 of 2,664 images have no alt text, spread over 8 pages.
WatchIndexing104 pages indexed. 10 real pages are not; 5 of them were improved and sent to Google today. The rest are feeds, archives and admin URLs, which is correct.
WatchBroken linksNo broken links in visible page content. The 20 dead internal links all sit in a hidden link block on the home page, and they make up the 8% of Google's crawl that hits 404 pages.
WatchExternal links/phoenix-sewer-line-problems/ links to another plumbing company's site. Two .gov/.edu sources could not be checked from here (region-blocked).
WatchServer responseGoogle measures 843 ms average response. My crawl saw a 1.5 s median for the HTML alone.
FixMobile speedAll 65 mobile URLs Google has data for are "Needs improvement": the main content takes longer than 2.5 s to appear. None are rated good.

What to do next

Look at the Business Profile listing on a phone

The listing's clicks and calls fell while views held. Check what a searcher sees today: the website button, hours, photos, the latest post (none published since Jul 7; two are waiting in review) and the newest reviews. This is the single biggest source of lost clicks.

Business Profile ownerThis week

Review the Search ad campaign

Ad clicks fell from 240 a week (Aug 31) to 52 and then 21, while cost per click rose from about $2 to about $24 in the week of Sep 14. Website conversion tracking has also been silent since late July, so the campaign is bidding without lead data.

Ads managerThis week

Speed up mobile pages

Turn on LiteSpeed's image and CSS optimizations (JS Minify stays off, it breaks the footer) and shrink the three large self-hosted videos. This lifts every page at once.

WebsiteNeeds approval

Remove the hidden dead links on the home page

The 20 dead links come from an OTTO link block that has been frozen since its crawl was switched off. Stripping it from the page output clears the 404s Google keeps crawling. The home page is protected, so this waits for approval.

WebsiteNeeds approval

Replace the competitor link

Swap the outbound link on /phoenix-sewer-line-problems/ for a .gov or .edu source. That page has shown in Performance Max ads, so it also waits for approval.

WebsiteNeeds approval

Judge progress by clicks, not total impressions

Track clicks, page-1 impressions and Business Profile actions. Total impressions will stay low while deep listings are not counted, and that number has little to do with leads. Recheck the 5 pages sent for indexing on Oct 1 to 3, and the full baseline on Oct 9.
